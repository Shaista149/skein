Notation guide
MR:6 sc into a magic ring
ch:21 chain 21
chr:6 chain ring - like MR but with no center hub cinching it closed
6sc or sc6 6 single crochet
hdc / dc / tr half-double crochet, double crochet, and treble crochet (same either-side count pattern as sc: 6dc or dc6)
slst slip stitch
sk12 or skip12 skip 12 stitches
turn flips the work at the end of a row, for flat pieces (chain)
inc increase (2sc in 1 stitch)
dec decrease (sc2tog, sc 2 stitches together into 1)
[sc, inc] x 6 repeat group
blo, 12sc back loop only
flo, 12sc front loop only
puff / hdc3puff puff stitch
bobble / dc3bobble bobble stitch
hdc3fan 3 separate hdc all worked into the same one stitch (e.g. a ruffle: "[hdc3fan] x 16", see the leg_with_ruffle preset). Works with any base/count: dc3fan, hdc2fan, sc4fan...
CC:coral sets the working color going forward, or changes color mid-row (see the striped and cc_midrow_demo presets). Accepts a color name (), a hex code like CC:#e8a598, or plain 0-255 numbers like CC:rgb232-165-152. Easiest way: click the button above the pattern box and pick visually instead of typing any of this by hand
sew closed (or fold) folds the end row of a piece in half and joins the halves together
sc closed (shorthand: scclose) similar to sew closed, but single crochets the halves together
pull closed fastens off, gathering the last round's stitches into a point (a magic ring in reverse)
fo fasten off, ends the piece. Nothing can follow it in the same component, pattern must end there
attach:r5-blo, 12sc reattach yarn into round 5's skipped back loop (see the loop_reattach_demo and leg_with_ruffle presets)
fuse:leg1+ch2+leg2, 36sc "crochet" (fuse) 2 components together - legs left unfinished then worked straight into the body. Name any saved piece from your list; ch2 adds a chain between leg1 and leg2. A full round counts as working stitches into the chain on both sides: if leg1 = leg2 = 16sc, the stitches count like 16 + 2 + 16 + 2 (16sc around leg1, 2sc into the back side of the chain, 16sc into leg2, 2sc in the front side of the chain, working from right to left). A fuse round can take a color the same way any round can: fuse:leg1+ch2+leg2, CC:coral, 36sc
10sc, graft:leg, 10sc crochet a component that ended in sew / sc closed into another structure. To test, try replacing round 19 in bunny_body with: 10sc, graft:bunny_ear, 10sc - this reads as 10sc, crochet the bunny_ear into the bunny_body for the next 4sc (the ear has 4sc available on the fold), then 10sc
mount:arm,r12:5 on its own line, after round 12 - attaches a separately-solved saved piece ("arm"), centered on round 12's 5th stitch. Add @20 (e.g. mount:arm,r12:5@20) to angle it 20 degrees from its resting position. Add !flat to press it flat and lay it along the surface instead of protruding (an ear/patch); add !flip if it lands facing/hanging the wrong of its two possible ways round - e.g. mount:ear,r12:5!flat!flip (see the bunny preset)
mount:ear2,r5:5-r7:8 spans the piece across two rows instead of one point - only works on a piece that ends sew closed / sc closed (a real seam with its own first/last stitch to lay diagonally). Both ends need an exact row AND stitch. Same @angle/!flip tags as above apply
